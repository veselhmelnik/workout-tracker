Privacy Policy for Setline

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Setline is a workout tracking application designed to work locally on your device.

1. Data stored by Setline

Setline stores workout-related information that you enter into the app, including:

  • workout routines
  • exercises
  • sets
  • repetitions
  • weights
  • workout sessions
  • workout history

This information is stored locally on your device.

2. Data collection

Setline does not require an account and does not send your workout routines, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, or workout history to a Setline server.

The current version of Setline does not provide cloud synchronization or cloud backup.

3. Data sharing

Setline does not sell your workout data.

The current version does not share your workout history with third parties.

4. External services

When you choose to search for exercise technique, Setline may open an external website or application.

Information you provide to or activity you perform within that external service is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Workout data remains stored locally on your device until you delete it through the application where such functionality is available, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application.

Uninstalling Setline may permanently remove locally stored workout data.

6. Backup and export

Cloud backup, synchronization, and data export are not currently available.

If these features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

7. Security

Setline is designed to keep workout data stored locally on your device.

Because the current version does not transmit workout history to a Setline server, your data remains under the storage and security protections provided by your device.

8. Children

Setline is not specifically designed for children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Setline's functionality changes.

The current version will always be available on this page.
